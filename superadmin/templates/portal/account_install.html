Install the app

Open the portal from your home screen or dock, in its own window. It still needs a connection: nothing about your work is stored on the device.

iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: in Chrome, open the menu and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

On a computer, use Install in the address bar of Chrome or Edge, or File, then Add to Dock in Safari on a Mac.
